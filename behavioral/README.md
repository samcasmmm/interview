# Behavioral & HR Interview Preparation

HR interview questions, behavioral scenarios (STAR method), leadership, and cultural fit.

## Topics to Cover
- **STAR Method**: Situation, Task, Action, Result
- **Self-Introduction & Career Story**: "Tell me about yourself" and trajectory walkthrough
- **Conflict & Disagreement**: Handling technical disagreements, difficult stakeholders, or scope pushback
- **Failure & Resilience**: Production incidents, mistakes made, lessons learned, and post-mortems
- **Leadership & Mentorship**: Guiding juniors, code review culture, driving engineering quality
- **Questions for the Interviewer**: Evaluating company engineering culture, architecture, and team dynamics
